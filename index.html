Our Products

Complete Fire Protection Range

From metal fire doors to architectural systems, we manufacture and supply the full spectrum of fire safety products.

Metal Fire Rated Door

Single leaf, equal leaf, and un-equal leaf doors with 120 minutes fire rating. Steel frame profile with galvanized steel construction.

120 min Rating 46mm Shutter BS 476

Glazed Fire Rated Door

Fire rated doors with 2-hour fire rated clear glass panels. Available in 60 and 120 minutes ratings with mineral wool insulation.

60/120 min Fire Rated Glass 1.6mm Steel

Glazed Door with Fixed Partition

Full glazed fire rated door systems with fixed partitions. Ideal for large openings requiring fire separation with visibility.

60/120 min Fixed Partition IS 3614

Steel Lead Lined Door

Nuclear and radiation protected doors for radiology and nuclear medicine applications. Built to AERB/DRS standards with 2mm lead sheet lining.

Radiation Shield AERB/DRS 2mm Lead

Fire Hose Cabinet Doors

Powder coated cabinet doors in PO Red with float glass vision panels. Available in single and double door configurations.

Powder Coated Vision Panel 1200x2000mm

HMPS Doors

Hollow Metal Pressed Steel Doors with steel frame profile design. Powder coated or paint finish in desired shade.

44/46mm Pressed Steel SS 304 Hinges

Fire Safety Standards

Fire Rating Indicative Chart

As per the 2005 Revised National Building Code of India - Part 4

S.No Description and Use of Opening Wall Rating (Hrs) Door Rating (Min) — BS 476 Frame Rating (Min) — EN 1634 / IS 3614
1 Walls that separate buildings or divide a single building into designated fire areas 4 120 120
2 Enclosures of vertical communication through buildings, such as stairwells and elevator shafts 2 60 120
3 Door assemblies dividing occupancies in a building 1 60 120
4 Opening in corridors or room partitions 1 30 60
5 Opening in walls with potential severe fire exposures from exterior of building 2 60 120
6 Walls with potential moderate fire exposure from exterior of building 1 30 60

Key Regulation: Fire doors with 2 hrs. resistance shall be provided at appropriate places along the escape route and particularly at the entrance to lift lobby and stair well where a 'funnel or flue effect' may be created (Clause 4.2.9, NBC 2005).
